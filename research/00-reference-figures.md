# 00 - Reference Figures (canonical values for the website)

*Research date: 2026-10-25 (index pass of 2026-10-25 added rows 52-62 and log entries 23-37). This file is the single source of truth for headline numbers. When a topic file (01-18, 20) gives a different value, the difference is a scope or vintage difference explained in section 3; the website uses the value below.*

## 1. Conventions

| Rule | Convention |
|---|---|
| Vintage | The **latest official vintage** as of 2026-10-25. For 2025 public-finance aggregates, that is the **October 2026 EDP notification** (Eurostat release of 21 Oct 2026, prepared by Insee), which supersedes Insee's March and May 2026 publications where they differ. |
| Accounting basis | ESA 2010 national accounts (*comptabilité nationale*), Maastricht definitions for deficit and debt, unless a row says "budget accounting" (*comptabilité budgétaire*, State only). |
| Euro area | **EA21** (fixed current composition, Bulgaria included from 2026). It is used for time series because its membership does not change across years. Eurostat's 2025 news release uses **EA20** (the composition in force in 2025); EA20 values are given in brackets where they differ by 0.1 pp or more. |
| Aggregates vs means | "EU-27 / EA21 aggregate" = GDP-weighted (sum of euros / sum of GDP). "Mean" and "median" = unweighted, across member states. |
| Ranks | Rank 1 = **highest value**, unless the row says "lowest". |
| Units | Every ratio says **% of GDP** or **% of total public expenditure (TE)**. EUR bn = billions of euros, current prices. PPS = purchasing power standards (EU-27 = 1). |
| *Prélèvements obligatoires* (PO) | *Prélèvements obligatoires* (compulsory levies) are Insee's concept: taxes plus actual social contributions, **net of tax credits**. They differ from OECD "total tax revenue" and from ESA "total revenue" (TR). The three are never interchanged. |
